How it works

01 · You own the identity

A new Ed25519 signing key is created on your device. Export an encrypted backup to recover it.

02 · You define the boundaries

Grant only one specific task type, with an expiry and a maximum number of uses.

03 · The agent proposes; you approve

Local task tools produce a preview. Nothing external is sent. Approved work receives a signed, independently verifiable receipt.

Your signing identity

Accountability

Signed decision history

Receipts bind a decision to the grant, its exact proposal hash, and a timestamp. Export one and verify it independently in any supported browser.

Verify an exported receipt

Verification uses the public key embedded in the receipt and requires no account or server. It verifies cryptographic integrity, not real-world identity or external execution.
