Bring your own AI agent.

Use an AI agent from any provider or one you built yourself. Smaran Agent is meant to give it a temporary, limited pass instead of your account key. The AI itself runs elsewhere.

Example: Your email agent may read selected messages until tomorrow, but it must not send or delete anything. The email integration must check the permission before acting.

Available now: Smaran sign-in and a locally verified signed-pass demonstration. Not available yet: connecting your own agent, real email/calendar actions, MagicBlock-enforced limits or shared revocation. The demonstration only formats notes.
