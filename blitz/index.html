SMARAN

One username and password.
No wallet. No seed phrase. No crypto.

Make one account and use it in every game below, on your phone or your computer. It is free, there is nothing to buy, and what you win stays yours: a relic you earn in Magic Over also shows in EternalBliss and SmaranWorld.

Pick an app

  • Magic OverCricket duels against the computer or a friend. One four or six earns a relic.
  • EternalBlissAn adventure game with quests, battles and shared worlds.
  • SmaranWorldA shared world to walk and chat in. Relic holders keep its 144 villages.
  • Ludo FreeflowLudo with no turns: everyone moves at once. Online dice are drawn fairly for every roll.
  • StreaksNot a game: keep a daily habit, one check-in a day.

How it works
Your account is a key on Solana, locked with your password and kept on Solana itself, so the same login works everywhere and there is no login server to shut down. Games run on MagicBlock, which makes every move instant and free for you. More about the login.
