Good to know

  • A streak shows that you checked in, not that you did the habit. It is a promise to yourself.
  • Your day starts at midnight in the time zone of your first check-in, and that stays fixed, so changing it cannot win back a missed day.
  • Streaks are public, under your username, and only you can check in as you.
  • Check-ins are free. They run on MagicBlock and are saved on Solana's test network at most once an hour. A test network can be reset.
